# ADR-0013: Hierarchical Reusable Model and Library Taxonomy

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-16
- Decision owners: Project maintainers
- Related requirements: `REQ-005`, `REQ-023`, `REQ-040`, `REQ-048`, `REQ-051`, `REQ-052`, `REQ-053`, `REQ-059`
- Extends: `ADR-0001`, `ADR-0004`, `ADR-0005`

## Context

A component-only registry cannot express the reusable path from laws and primitive kernels to vendor devices, boards, systems, projects, and evidence. [Source PDF, pp. 2, 13-21, 26-29, 31-37, 40]

## Decision

The library shall distinguish physics, primitive, composite, behavioral, and external-adapter models; generic and vendor devices; symbols; packages; pin profiles and bindings; boards/modules; systems; user projects; and validation/simulation artifacts. `behavioral` is the canonical enum; `behavioural` is a recognized import/display alias only.

References used by published records and runs shall resolve to immutable revision IDs plus content hashes. Composite and composition graphs shall be acyclic, type-compatible, dependency-closed, and traversable in both directions. Lineage shall cover principle-to-result and result-to-principle traversal.

## Consequences

- Reusable generic behavior avoids copying a whole internal circuit per ordering code.
- Boards and systems become composition records, not oversized component or package records.
- Registry counts report only actual enumerated records.

## Rejected alternatives

- **One table/type for every artifact:** erases invariant boundaries and makes validation ambiguous.
- **Mutable latest-version references in results:** destroys reproducibility.

## Compliance

Collapsing taxonomy layers or allowing cyclic or unresolved published dependency graphs requires a superseding ADR.
