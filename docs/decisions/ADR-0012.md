# ADR-0012: R4 Applied Physics and Real-World Fidelity Gate

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-16
- Decision owners: Project maintainers
- Related requirements: `REQ-013`, `REQ-014`, `REQ-015`, `REQ-016`, `REQ-039`, `REQ-043`, `REQ-044`, `REQ-045`, `REQ-046`, `REQ-047`
- Extends: `ADR-0001`, `ADR-0005`, `ADR-0006`

## Context

R4 was named “Non-ideal, thermal, tolerance, and failure.” That scope remains required but its name did not communicate the full real-world fidelity gate demanded before digital/mixed-signal and the production MVP. [Source PDF, pp. 5-12, 27-30, 33, 37, 39-41]

## Decision

R4 is renamed **Applied Physics and Real-World Fidelity**. Its existing tolerance, Monte Carlo, parasitic, leakage, noise, power-limit, electrothermal, derating, aging, failure, visualization, injection, and deterministic-seed scope is preserved. R4 additionally governs formal model specifications; dimensions; validity; provenance; accuracy and uncertainty; physical correlation; environment; manufacturing distributions and mismatch; interconnection; source; and instrument physics.

Gate G4 is a hard predecessor of G5 and the G6 Realistic Electronics MVP. R4 passes only with schema-valid models, deterministic statistical evidence, declared envelopes, current evidence for `GRC-021`, `GRC-022`, and `GRC-026`, the new physical benchmark fixtures, failure-path evidence, and a release audit showing no unbounded scientific claim. The specifications for `GRC-023` through `GRC-025` remain part of the preserved R4-originated scope, but their catalog-bound execution evidence remains scheduled for R7; they cannot become a circular prerequisite for G4.

## Consequences

- R0 through R13 remain exactly fourteen releases; R5 through R13 are not renumbered and R14 is not added.
- Later catalog work may add family-specific R4 evidence, but it cannot weaken G4.
- Experimental correlation is planned and versioned separately from differential simulator comparison.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Create a new release after R13:** breaks stable roadmap IDs.
- **Move the old R4 scope elsewhere:** loses compatibility and traceability.

## Compliance

Bypassing G4, restoring the old R4 name as normative, or weakening its required evidence requires a superseding ADR and release migration analysis.
