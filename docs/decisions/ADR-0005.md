# ADR-0005: Fidelity Levels F0 Through F5

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-11
- Decision owners: Project maintainers
- Related requirements: `REQ-001`, `REQ-002`, `REQ-014`, `REQ-015`, `REQ-016`, `REQ-023`, `REQ-028`

## Context

The source brief distinguishes device physics, circuit/SPICE, switch, gate, RTL, architecture, and functional simulation and recommends changing abstraction with scale. [Source PDF, pp. 3-4, 13-21, 27]

The registry and UI need a small, stable vocabulary that expresses model detail without conflating visual appearance with accuracy.

## Decision

Every executable or structural component binding shall declare one or more of these fidelity levels:

| Level | Normative meaning |
|---|---|
| `F0` | Connectivity only: pins, nets, hierarchy, domains, and structural checks; no behavioral result. |
| `F1` | Ideal or equation model: analytical or deliberately simplified behavior. |
| `F2` | Behavioral or digital-timing model: functional, event, switch, or timing behavior with declared delays and states. |
| `F3` | SPICE compact or macro-model: nonlinear circuit behavior validated against a declared SPICE or measured reference envelope. |
| `F4` | Electrothermal, tolerance, and failure model: coupled temperature, variation, ratings, degradation, or failure behavior. |
| `F5` | Physical or TCAD research model: device geometry, fields, carriers, doping, or related research physics, normally on backend/HPC resources. [Source PDF, pp. 3-4, 14] |

- A higher number does not automatically mean a better answer; model capability, parameter range, validation, and the user's question determine suitability.
- A model may support several analyses at different levels, but each binding must declare that mapping explicitly.
- User-facing modes such as Fast, Balanced, Accurate, or Research may select policies, but the selected F-level remains visible. [Source PDF, p. 27]
- Schematic symbols, physical component renderings, and IC packages do not determine fidelity.
- Unsupported fidelity or analysis combinations shall fail clearly and shall not silently fall back.

## Consequences

- Component, project, task, test, and result records can use one fidelity vocabulary.
- Abstraction switching requires compatible boundary semantics and documented state conversion.
- Accuracy claims remain model-specific even when two models share an F-level.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Names such as realistic or accurate without levels:** hide important differences and limitations.
- **One monotonic accuracy score:** cannot represent different analysis capabilities.
- **Visual detail as fidelity:** confuses rendering with physical validation.

## Compliance

Changing an F-level's meaning or adding a new canonical level requires a superseding ADR and migration analysis.
