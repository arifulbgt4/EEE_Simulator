# ADR-0018: Immutable Revisions and Dependency-Safe Library Operations

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-16
- Decision owners: Project maintainers
- Related requirements: `REQ-052`, `REQ-053`, `REQ-059`, `REQ-060`, `REQ-061`, `REQ-062`
- Extends: `ADR-0004`, `ADR-0009`

## Context

Mutable published definitions and unrestricted deletion would make saved projects, simulations, citations, and reverse dependencies irreproducible. [Source PDF, pp. 26-28, 31-32, 40-42]

## Decision

Published library records shall be immutable, content-addressed revisions with stable logical IDs. Change creates a new revision; deprecation or supersession names a replacement and compatibility statement. Referenced or published revisions cannot be hard-deleted. Archive and soft deletion hide discovery while preserving exact resolution.

The Library Service shall support validated domain operations for draft, revise, compare, publish, deprecate, supersede, archive, soft-delete, restore, duplicate, dependency and reverse-dependency traversal, reference migration, import, and export. Writes require authorization, validation, provenance/license policy, optimistic concurrency, idempotency where applicable, and audit records.

## Consequences

- Historical projects and evidence remain resolvable.
- Safe retirement may require migration plans and reverse-dependency review.
- Publication has stricter permission and evidence gates than private drafting.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Update published rows in place:** invalidates prior results.
- **Cascade hard delete:** destroys dependency history.

## Compliance

Mutable publication or hard deletion of a referenced revision requires rejection or a superseding ADR.
