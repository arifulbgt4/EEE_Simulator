# ADR-0011: Applied Physics First

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-16
- Decision owners: Project maintainers
- Related requirements: `REQ-001`, `REQ-002`, `REQ-039`, `REQ-040`, `REQ-041`, `REQ-042`, `REQ-043`, `REQ-044`
- Extends: `ADR-0001`, `ADR-0005`

## Context

The platform already uses several fidelity levels, but a fidelity ladder alone does not prove that its models remain connected to physical principles, units, evidence, and limits. The source brief builds from electrical laws and device behavior toward circuits and computers and identifies model accuracy as a primary risk. [Source PDF, pp. 1-12, 27-30, 39-41]

## Decision

The project shall describe itself as an **Applied Physics-driven, hierarchical, multi-fidelity electronics and computer simulation platform**. Its normative development and evidence chain is:

`Physical Principles -> Mathematical Models -> Engineering Equations -> Numerical Algorithms -> Primitive Models -> Composite Models -> Library Devices -> Circuits and Boards -> Complete Systems -> Experimental Validation`.

Physics informs F0 through F5. Earlier releases may use documented idealizations; R4 adds the required real-world electrical, thermal, environmental, manufacturing, aging, interconnection, source, instrument, uncertainty, and failure effects. Every published model shall retain units, validity, provenance, uncertainty, limitations, dependencies, and validation evidence.

## Consequences

- Product, architecture, model, task, and validation documents share one scientific lineage.
- A mathematically working implementation is not evidence of physical accuracy.
- Claims are bounded by a declared accuracy envelope and evidence state.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Introduce physics only in R4:** contradicts the foundation of circuit simulation.
- **Use realistic-looking components as proof of fidelity:** appearance, dimensional accuracy, and model validity are separate concerns.

## Compliance

Removing the Applied Physics chain, weakening evidence requirements, or treating physical correlation as optional for a claimed envelope requires a superseding ADR.
